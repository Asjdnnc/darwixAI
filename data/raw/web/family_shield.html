FamilyShield: one plan for the whole household

Updated 03/05/2026

Who is covered

Family Shield covers the policyholder, a spouse or domestic partner, and up to four dependent children under age 26 on one policy.

Children can be added within 30 days of birth or adoption without waiting for the annual enrollment window.

Costs at a glance

Premium amount starts from $520 per month for a family of four. The plan has a $6,000 family deductible ($3,000 per person) and a $14,000 family out-of-pocket maximum.

Final premiums depend on the age, location, and medical underwriting results of every person on the policy.

Getting a family quote

To prepare a family quote, the advisor needs the age of every family member, the state of residence, tobacco use for each adult, and any pre existing disease for each person.

A family quote is an estimate only and does not guarantee coverage for prior medical conditions.
