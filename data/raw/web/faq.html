Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 5th Mar 2026

Is there a waiting period before my coverage starts?

Yes. There is a 30-day waiting time for non-emergency services from the policy start date. Accidents and emergencies are covered from day one. Planned joint replacement and cataract surgery have a 12-month waiting period.

Are pre-existing conditions covered?

Pre-existing illnesses must be declared on the application. Depending on health underwriting, a condition may be covered in full, covered with a specific exclusion, or covered with an additional premium. An advisor cannot confirm the outcome during a call.

How do I make a claim?

At in-network providers, claims are cashless: show your member card and the provider bills Darwix Health directly. For out-of-network emergency care, submit a reimbursement claim with itemized bills within 90 days of discharge. Complete claims are processed within 15 business days.

Can I cancel my policy?

Every new policy has a 30-day free-look period. If you cancel within 30 days of the policy start date and have made no claims, you receive a full premium refund.

What happens if I miss a premium payment?

There is a 30-day grace period after the premium due date. Coverage continues during the grace period. If the premium is not paid by the end of the grace period, the policy lapses.

In which states are plans available?

Plans are currently offered in Texas, Florida, Georgia, Arizona, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Michigan, Tennessee, Colorado, and Virginia. Plans are not currently offered in New York or California.
