Essential Care

Essential Care is an individual health plan for adults aged 18 to 64. Monthly fee starts from $189 for a 30-year-old non-tobacco user.

The plan has a $3,000 annual deductable and a $8,000 out of pocket limit. Primary care visits cost a $30 copay and generic prescriptions cost a $15 copay.

Care is covered at network hospitals and in-network clinics. Out-of-network care is covered only for emergencies.

Family Shield

Family Shield covers the policyholder, a spouse or domestic partner, and up to four dependant children under age 26 on a single policy.

Premium amount starts from $520 per month for a family of four. The plan has a $6,000 family deductible ($3,000 per person) and a $14,000 family out-of-pocket maximum.

Pediatric dental and vision care, maternity care, and newborn care are included.

Senior Secure

Senior Secure is designed for adults aged 55 to 75 who want lower out-of-pocket costs. Premium starts from $260 per month.

The plan has a $1,500 deductible and a $5,000 OOP max. Annual preventive screenings and a yearly wellness visit are included at no extra cost.

Paying your premium

Premiums can be paid monthly, quarterly, or annually. Members who pay annually receive a 5% discount on the total yearly premium.
