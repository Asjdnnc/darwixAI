# Darwix Life Philippines — Premium Payment Channels

Owner: Customer Servicing | Last updated: February 10, 2026

## Over-the-counter bank partners

Premiums may be paid at any branch of BPI, BDO, Metrobank, Landbank, or Security Bank using the client's policy number as reference. Over-the-counter payments post within two (2) banking days.

## E-wallets and online

GCash and Maya accept premium payments under the Bills Payment menu, Insurance category. Online banking transfers are accepted through InstaPay and PESONet. E-wallet payments post within one (1) banking day.

## Non-bank payment centers

Payments are accepted at Bayad Center, SM Bills Payment, Palawan Express, and participating 7-Eleven CLiQQ kiosks. A service fee may be charged by the payment centre and is not collected by Darwix Life.

## Auto-debit arrangement

Clients may enrol in auto-debit from a bank account or credit card. Enrolment takes one (1) full billing cycle to take effect, so the premium falling due within that cycle must still be paid manually.

## Posting and confirmation

Payment posting is not instant. A client who has already paid within the last two banking days should be thanked and the call ended; the system will reflect the payment once posted. Official receipts are sent by email and are viewable in the client portal.

## What to confirm before ending a payment call

Confirm the amount due, the due date, the chosen payment channel, and the client's preferred contact number. Never ask for a card number, CVV, OTP, account password, or a screenshot of a one-time PIN.
