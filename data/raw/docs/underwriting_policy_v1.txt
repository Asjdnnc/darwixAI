1. PURPOSE
This policy defines how advisors qualify health insurance leads.

2. MANDATORY DISCLOSURES
Before discussing any plan price, the agent must state that premiums may
change after underwriting.

3. ELIGIBILITY RULES
Essential Care is available to applicants aged 21 to 60. Senior Secure is
available to applicants aged 60 to 70. A 60-day waiting period applies to
all non-emergency services.
