1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the rules that advisors and automated agents must fol-
low when qualifying health insurance leads by phone. It replaces version
1.0 in full.

2. MANDATORY DISCLOSURES
At the start of every call the agent must state that the call is recorded
for quality and compliance purposes.
Before discussing any plan price, the agent must state that final coverage
and premiums are subject to medical underwriting and depend on verified
medical history.
If the customer does not consent to recording, the agent must end the
automated conversation and offer a callback from a human advisor.

3. ELIGIBILITY RULES
Essential Care is available to applicants aged 18 to 64. Family Shield is
available to a policyholder aged 18 to 64 together with eligible depen-
dants. Senior Secure is available to applicants aged 55 to 75.
Applicants must live in a state where plans are offered: Texas, Florida,
Georgia, Arizona, North Carolina, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Illinois, Michigan,
Tennessee, Colorado, or Virginia.

4. PRELIMINARY LEAD QUALIFICATION
A lead is preliminarily qualified when the caller's age is within the
range of at least one plan, the caller lives in a state where plans are
offered, and the caller consents to be contacted.
A lead must be referred to a senior health advisor when the caller
reports cancer treatment in the last 5 years, kidney dialysis, an organ
transplant, or a current hospital admission.
A lead is not eligible for a quote when the caller lives outside the
offered states or no plan matches the caller's age. Offer to notify the
caller when plans become available in their state.
Agents provide a preliminary outcome only. Final eligibility is decided
by medical underwriting after a complete application.

5. PROHIBITED STATEMENTS
Agents must never guarantee approval or coverage of a pre-existing condi-
tion, quote a final premium, give medical advice or diagnoses, disparage
another insurer, or pressure the customer to decide during the call.

6. HUMAN ESCALATION
Transfer the caller to a licensed human advisor when the caller asks for
a person, raises a complaint, asks a detailed medical or legal question,
or disputes a claim on an existing policy.
Human advisors are available Monday to Friday 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. Central
Time and Saturday 9 a.m. to 2 p.m. Central Time. Outside these hours,
schedule a callback within 1 business day.
