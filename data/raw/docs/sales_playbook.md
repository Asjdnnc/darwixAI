# Darwix Health Phone Sales Playbook

Owner: Sales Enablement | Last updated: March 12, 2026

## Call opening script

Greet the caller, introduce yourself as a Darwix Health advisor, and say that the call is recorded for quality and compliance purposes. Ask for permission to continue. Ask what prompted the caller to look for health coverage today.

## Mandatory disclosure reminder

Before discussing any plan price, the agent must state that final coverage and premiums are subject to medical underwriting and depend on verified medical history.

## Qualification questions

Ask one question at a time, in this order: the caller's age, state of residence, how many people need coverage and their ages, tobacco use, any current medical conditions, current insurance, the best time for a callback, and consent to be contacted. If an answer conflicts with an earlier answer, politely read back both answers and ask the caller which one is correct. Never guess a missing detail.

## Objection: It is too expensive

Acknowledge the concern and ask what monthly budget the caller has in mind. Mention that paying annually gives a 5% discount and that Essential Care has a lower starting premium than Family Shield. Never promise a discount or price that is not in the approved plan materials.

## Objection: I already have coverage through my employer

Respect the existing coverage and ask whether any family members are not covered by the employer plan. Family Shield can cover dependents who are not on an employer plan. Do not pressure the caller to cancel existing coverage.

## Objection: I need to talk to my spouse first

Agree that it is a family decision. Offer a callback at a time when both partners are available and offer to send a written plan summary in the meantime.

## Objection: Insurance companies never pay claims

Acknowledge the frustration. Explain that claims at in-network providers are cashless and that complete reimbursement claims are processed within 15 business days. Never guarantee that a specific claim will be approved.

## Objection: I do not want to share medical information on the phone

Explain that health details are needed only so medical underwriting can give an accurate premium. The caller may skip the question and complete it later through the secure application or with a licensed advisor.

## Objection: Is this a scam?

Stay calm and do not argue. Offer to email the written plan documents and the company licence number so the caller can verify them independently, and offer a callback from a licensed advisor.
